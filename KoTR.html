Knights of the Rollerblades


Beat your friends in this medieval-pinball-arcade game





Idisen

Idisen is a non-profit organization started by female developers and aimed at helping female developers to succeed and thrive within the game industry.

In 2025, I had the pleasure of joining the 9 day Idisen Game Jam. Together with 5 other developers, we created this pinball jousting fest!



Gameplay Programming:

  • The rollerblading mechanic
  • Player movement, including boosting and jumping
  • Attacking targets and opponents via the Jousting system

Stystems Programming:

  • Multiplayer implementation
  • Background lighting system
  • Dual input support

UI Programming:

  • Character selection screen
  • Scoreboard, points and crown system
  • Victory screen

I would also like to give a big thanks to Idisen, Merel Steeland and Goedele Godfroid for organizing this iniative and spearheading the female game developer space in Belgium.
